Proposed solution

1. Structural Inpainting
LaMa evaluates the spatial frequencies surrounding damaged areas, synthesizing missing content and structural boundaries flawlessly at native resolution.
2. Chromatic Reconstruction
DeOldify & Noisy GAN apply historical context to assign realistic, period-accurate colors without bleeding across sharp chromatic edges.
3. Super-Resolution
Real-ESRGAN acts like a gardener revealing hidden detail, synthesizing entirely new high-frequency textures to eliminate noise and upscale to 4K clarity.
4. Face Restoration
CodeFormer maps faces to a high-quality latent space, correcting anatomical distortions while seamlessly compositing features back into the high-res background.
